WELCOME, EXPLORER

Make it your adventure.

01   Explore

Use the joystick, WASD or arrow keys. You can also tap the ground to walk. Approach a roaming creature and press Encounter.

02   Befriend

Weaken a creature before throwing an orb. Tap Quick strike to build energy, then unleash your signature move. Potions restore health. Guard before a telegraphed heavy attack; switch companions to change your elemental advantage.

03   Evolve

Battles earn experience. At levels 7 and 12, your companion can evolve into a bigger, more powerful form. Check the Team menu.

04   Discover

Earn six guardian sigils in order: Grove, Tide, Ember, Storm, Stone, Ancient. Guardians cannot be caught. Visit camp to heal and restock; glowing pickups give more orbs.

Progress saves automatically on this browser. This is an original, single-player creature adventure.
